    Save rules — read before you start

    • No mission replay. Keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter (15 manual slots, 1 autosave). Rotate the remaining slots for free-roam cleanup.
    • Cheats disable trophies and saving. Keep cheats off for the whole run. (PowerPyx)
    • One missable. The Chapter 1 balcony meditation for Positive Mental Attitude — see the Missables tab. Everything else is available in post-story free roam.
    • Quest givers go quiet, then return. Kenton and others vanish for stretches of the story; all trophy-related characters are available again later. Optional missions that expire do not matter — there are far more than 100.
